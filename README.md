# 🧖🏽‍♀️ Wig Sales & Profit Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based sales analytics project built using a practice wig sales dataset.

The goal was to demonstrate the complete data analytics workflow, from working with raw and inconsistent data to cleaning, exploring, analyzing, visualizing, and communicating business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

The project simulates a wig business and focuses on understanding sales performance, revenue, profitability, product demand, and quarterly trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which wig types are purchased the most?
- Which wig types generate the highest revenue?
- Which wig types generate the highest profit?
- How many transactions were recorded?
- How many wigs were sold?
- How is revenue performing across quarters?
- What business decisions can be supported by the data?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The raw dataset contained several inconsistencies that needed to be addressed before analysis.

The cleaning process included:

- Standardizing customer names
- Removing unnecessary spaces
- Standardizing wig names
- Correcting date formats
- Handling missing values
- Standardizing numerical and currency formats
- Checking for duplicate records
- Validating the cleaned dataset

The cleaned data was then used as the foundation for the analysis.

---

## 🔎 Data Analysis

Additional calculated fields were created to support the analysis:

### Revenue

`Quantity × Selling Price`

### Total Cost

`Quantity × Cost Price`

### Profit

`Revenue − Total Cost`

### Profit Margin

`Profit ÷ Revenue`

PivotTables were then used to summarize sales performance by wig type and across quarters.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several interesting findings:

- Closure Wig recorded the highest sales volume with **38 units sold**.
- Closure Wig generated the highest revenue at approximately **₦5.13M**.
- Human Hair generated the highest total profit at approximately **₦1.96M**.
- Q2 recorded the strongest quarterly revenue performance at approximately **₦11M**.
- Total revenue was approximately **₦30.13M**.
- Total profit was approximately **₦10.75M**.
- The analysis demonstrated that the best-selling product is not necessarily the most profitable product.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings:

1. Monitor inventory levels for high-demand wig categories to reduce potential stockouts.

2. Pay attention to high-profit products when making pricing, marketing, and inventory decisions.

3. Investigate the factors behind the strong Q2 revenue performance to determine whether similar strategies can be replicated.

4. Evaluate products using both sales volume and profitability rather than relying on sales quantity alone.
